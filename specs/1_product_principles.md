# Nestfolio — Volume 1: Product & Principles

## 1. Purpose
This volume defines the product vision, user philosophy, and guiding principles of Nestfolio. It serves as the conceptual foundation for all technical, regulatory, and operational volumes.

This document intentionally separates **what Nestfolio is** from **how it is implemented**.

---

## 2. Product Mission
Nestfolio is an AI‑managed investment platform acting as a digital financial coach for novice investors, simplifying investing through automation, transparency, and explainable decision-making.

---

## 3. Regulatory Positioning
Nestfolio operates under a **Platform + Licensed Partner Model**:
- Nestfolio provides AI infrastructure, orchestration, UX, and explainability.
- A licensed investment firm partner holds discretionary authority and regulatory responsibility.
- Assets remain custodied with Interactive Brokers.

This structure enables safe market entry while supporting future transition to independent authorization.

---

## 4. Target Users
- Novice and inexperienced investors
- Trust‑oriented savers
- Users seeking hands‑off automated investing

Primary launch market: Italy (EU-compliant expansion-ready).

---

## 5. Core User Experience Principles

### Trust Through Transparency
Users must understand *why* decisions occur.

### Clarity and Simplicity
Financial jargon is avoided; explanations use plain language.

### Customer‑Centric Automation
Automation minimizes required user action while maintaining perceived control.

### Localized Communication
Language, tone, and cultural framing align with the user’s market.

---

## 6. Operating Modes
Nestfolio provides configurable operating modes:
- Conservative
- Balanced (default)
- Aggressive

Modes define autonomy, guardrails, and communication behavior.

---

## 7. Communication Philosophy
Default behavior follows a configurable hybrid model:
- Autonomous actions explained post‑execution
- High‑impact actions pre‑notified
- User confirmations required where mandated

Users may override notification timing within compliance constraints.

---

## 8. Trust Model
Nestfolio reinforces confidence through:
- Explainable AI
- Auditability
- Predictable automation
- Clear ownership boundaries between platform and regulated partner
